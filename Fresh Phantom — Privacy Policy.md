# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 9, 2026**

Fresh Phantom respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you visit our website, contact us, or submit an inquiry; why we use it; and the choices available to you.

## Information You Give Us

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

- your name;
- email address;
- telephone number;
- company or organization;
- project information;
- budget or timeline information;
- files, links, or materials you choose to provide; and
- the contents of messages or inquiries you send us.

You are not required to provide more information than is reasonably necessary for your inquiry.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, passwords, private credentials, financial account information, health information, or confidential third-party information through our general website forms.

## Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our site and its hosting, security, or analytics providers may automatically receive limited technical information when you visit.

That information may include:

- IP address;
- browser and device type;
- operating system;
- referring page;
- pages visited;
- approximate geographic region;
- date and time of access;
- website performance information; and
- basic interaction or usage information.

We may use this information to operate, secure, understand, and improve the website.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for website functionality, security, analytics, preferences, or performance.

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies.

You can also control many cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect how some parts of the site work.

## How We Use Information

We may use information we receive to:

- respond to inquiries;
- communicate about potential projects;
- provide requested services;
- prepare proposals or project estimates;
- maintain business records;
- operate and improve our website;
- understand website traffic and performance;
- protect our systems against fraud, abuse, or security threats;
- comply with legal obligations; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

We do not use information submitted through an inquiry form to create a client relationship unless a separate engagement is agreed upon.

## How We Share Information

Fresh Phantom does **not sell your personal information**.

We may disclose limited information when reasonably necessary to organizations that help us operate our business, such as website hosting providers, cloud services, email providers, analytics services, form-processing services, security providers, professional advisers, and other contractors.

These providers may process information only as necessary to perform services for us and according to their applicable agreements and policies.

We may also disclose information:

- when required by law, subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process;
- when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, systems, or property;
- to investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity; or
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or transfer of some or all of the business.

## Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services.

Their privacy practices are controlled by them, not Fresh Phantom. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of services you choose to visit or use.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business records, or as required by law.

For example, we may retain project inquiries and related correspondence so we can respond, maintain appropriate business records, or reference previous discussions.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete or anonymize it.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect the information we maintain.

No website, network, transmission method, or storage system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information we maintain about you.

These may include rights to:

- request access to your information;
- request correction of inaccurate information;
- request deletion;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing depends on consent; or
- receive information about how your data is used or disclosed.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the information below.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising rights available to you under applicable privacy law.

## Marketing Communications

If Fresh Phantom sends promotional email communications, you may unsubscribe using the instructions contained in the message or by contacting us.

We may still send non-promotional communications relating to an active inquiry, project, transaction, or business relationship.

## Children's Privacy

This website and our professional services are not directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through this website.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, remove it.

## Visitors Outside the United States

Fresh Phantom operates from the United States.

If you access the site from another country, information you provide may be processed or stored in the United States or in other locations used by our service providers.

Privacy protections in those jurisdictions may differ from those where you live.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, technologies, or legal obligations change.

When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page. Material changes may be communicated in additional ways when appropriate or legally required.
